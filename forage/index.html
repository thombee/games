    How to play

    Six words fit today's clue. Find them in the grid.

    Words run in straight lines: across, down or diagonally, and sometimes backwards. Drag from the first letter to the last, or tap the first letter and then the last.

    Stuck? Hint lights up the first letter of a word you haven't found. The clock starts at your first touch, and a new grid arrives at midnight.

    With a keyboard: the arrow keys move between letters, and Space or Enter marks the first letter and then the last. Escape lets go.
