How to play

Moles are asleep under some of the lawn. Dig every square that has no mole, and don’t wake any.

A dug square shows how many of its eight neighbours, corners included, have a mole under them. A blank has none, and digs its neighbours for you.

Think a square hides a mole? Mark it with a daisy 🌼: switch to Mark under the lawn, press and hold the square, or right-click it. Tap a number that already has all its daisies to dig the rest of its neighbours at once.

Every lawn can be worked out without guessing. Stuck? A hint shows one square you can be sure of, and why.

Keyboard: arrow keys move, Space or Enter digs, F marks. A new lawn arrives at midnight: small and gentle on Monday, big on Sunday. One go a day.
