    A new ladder every day at midnight. The same words for everyone.

    How to play

    Get from the top word to the bottom word. Each rung changes one letter of the word above it, and every rung must be a real word.

    1. COLD
    2. CORD▲
    3. CARD▲
    4. WARD▲
    5. WARM
    • ▲ the word is closer to the bottom word
    • ▼ it is no closer: you may be wandering

    Hint puts in the next word of a shortest route. Step back takes your last word off. The fewest steps count everyday words only, but any real word is allowed.

    Keys: type a word and press Enter. Backspace deletes a letter. A new ladder arrives at midnight, the same for everyone.
