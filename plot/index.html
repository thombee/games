    A new plot every day at midnight. Same beds for everyone.

    How to play

    Four beds hide four vegetables. A vegetable can be in more than one bed. Work it out in six guesses.

    Tap vegetables to fill the beds, then press Dig. Tap a filled bed to empty it.

    🥕✓ 🍅• 🌽– 🎃–
    • ✓ Green: the carrot is in bed 1
    • • Yellow: a tomato is planted, in another bed
    • – Plain: no corn, and no pumpkin
    • ✓ A tick in the tray: planted somewhere

    With repeats, each planted vegetable lights up one bed of your guess. If the beds hold 🍅 🥕 🍆 🎃:

    🥕– 🥕✓ 🌽– 🫑–

    Right beds are counted first. The first carrot is plain because the only carrot is already found.

    Keys: 1 to 6 or C T O E A P place a vegetable, Backspace removes the last, Enter digs.

    Hard mode starts with tomorrow's plot.
