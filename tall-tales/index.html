    How to play

    Each card makes a claim about the natural world. Swipe right, or tap ✓ True, if you think it's true. Swipe left, or tap ✕ Tall tale, if you think it's made up.

    The card then turns over to show the answer and where it comes from.

    There are seven cards a day, the same for everyone, and new ones arrive at midnight.

    On a keyboard, the right arrow says True and the left arrow says Tall tale.
