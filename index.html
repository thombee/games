🎲 Arcade

Small daily games. A new one of each, every day. 12 games.

Knowledge 4

What you know, or can reason out, about food, animals and nature.

  • 🌱 Proteindle Which has more protein? Ten match-ups a day between two foods in everyday portions.
  • 🐔 Pecking Order Put five things in order, such as longest life to shortest, in three tries.
  • 🥘 Potluck Guess the dish from its ingredients, turned over one at a time, in six goes.
  • 🃏 Tall Tales Seven claims about the natural world: true, or a tall tale?

Logic puzzles 5

One puzzle a day with one answer, worked out from the rules. No knowledge needed.

  • 🥕 Plot Work out the vegetables in four garden beds in seven guesses, marked as in Wordle.
  • 🟡 Four Up A four-in-a-row position part-way through a game: find the winning move.
  • 🥬 Patch One seedling in each row, column and bed of a garden, with no two touching.
  • 🥔 Unearth A nonogram: fill squares from the row and column clues to uncover a picture.
  • 🌼 Molehill Dig the garden without waking the moles. Minesweeper made gentle, and never a forced guess.

Word 2

Finding and making words.

  • 🧺 Forage Six hidden words on one theme in a 7 by 7 word search, against the clock.
  • 🪜 Rung Change one letter at a time to turn one word into another. A daily word ladder.

Arcade 1

Quick play for a score. Three goes a day; the best one counts.

  • 🪴 Grow The rules of 2048 in a garden: merge plants into bigger ones in eighty moves.
Each game is served from its own repo, so changes show up on reload. Progress is kept in this browser, per game.
