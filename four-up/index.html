    How to play

    You play Yellow. Tap a column to drop a disc in it. Red plays back.

    Get four in a row (across, up or diagonally) within the number of moves shown, whatever Red does. Only one first move does it.

    If a move lets the win slip, Red shows how it escapes and the board goes back to the start. You get three tries.

    One puzzle a day, the same for everyone. Monday and Tuesday are win in 1, Wednesday to Friday win in 2, the weekend win in 3. On a keyboard, the arrow keys choose a column and Enter drops the disc. Yellow discs carry a ring, so the two colours differ by more than colour.
