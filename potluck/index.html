        How to play

        Work out today's dish from its ingredients. You start with one.

        Type a dish and pick it from the list, or press Skip. Each wrong guess or skip shows the next ingredient, and from the fourth the card also says which part of the world the dish comes from. You have six goes.

        • 🟩 The dish: you got it
        • 🟨 Wrong dish, but from the right part of the world
        • ⬜ Wrong dish, from somewhere else
        • ⬛ Skipped

        Everyone gets the same dish, and a new one arrives at midnight.
