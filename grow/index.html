    How to play

    Swipe the garden, or press an arrow key, and every plant slides that way as far as it can. Two of the same that meet merge and grow into the next stage:

    You get 80 moves a round. After each one a seed, or sometimes a sprout, comes up. Bigger plants score more, and each square left empty at the end of a round scores 20. If the bed fills up and nothing can merge, the round ends early.

    Three rounds a day; your best counts. Everyone gets the same garden today, and a new one comes up at midnight.
