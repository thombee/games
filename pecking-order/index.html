      How to play

      Put the five cards in the order the question asks for. Drag them, or use the arrows on each card. Then press Lock in.

      Cards in the right place turn green and stay put. Move the others and try again. You have three tries.

      There is one question a day, the same for everyone, and a new one arrives at midnight.

      Every figure comes from a public dataset, named with the answer. Animal lifespans are the longest on record; food figures are per 100 g or per kilogram, as each question says.
