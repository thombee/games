How to play

Slide things out of the way so today's animal can walk through the gate on the right and into the meadow.

Drag a piece along its length. Hay bales, logs, the trough and the tractor only slide the way they point, and stop when they meet something. One slide of one piece, however far, is one move.

Par is the fewest moves it can be done in. Finish in par or fewer for three stars, in up to one and a half times par for two, and any way at all for one.

Undo takes back a move. Hint shows a good next move. With a keyboard, Tab to a piece and slide it with the arrow keys. Or tap a piece to select it, then use the Move buttons under the yard. The first week starts gentle. After that, puzzles start gentle on Monday and get harder through the week. A new one arrives at midnight.
