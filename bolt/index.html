How to play

Today’s runaway is making a run for the hills. It runs by itself: tap anywhere on the field, or press Space or Enter, to jump. Tap again in the air for a second, smaller hop, for tractors, haystacks and fences in pairs. Under a low branch, keep your feet on the ground.

You get three runs a day and your best one counts. One tab plays at a time: while a run is on in another tab, this one waits for it to end. Everyone gets the same course today, and a new course and a new runaway arrive at midnight.

The run itself is a game of quick reactions played by sight, so it can’t be played with a screen reader. Your distances, results and stats are read out.
