How to play

A picture is hidden in the grid. The numbers beside each row and above each column are its clues: each number is a run of filled squares, in order, with a gap between runs. So 3 1 means three filled squares, at least one empty one, then one more.

Tap a square to fill it. Switch to ✕ Cross to mark squares you know are empty; crosses are only notes. Drag along a row or column to do several at once. A clue turns green when its line matches it.

Every picture can be worked out without guessing. Stuck? Check briefly marks any filled square that’s wrong, and Hint fills in a whole row or column. Both are counted.

Keyboard: arrow keys move, Space fills, X crosses, Delete clears, Ctrl+Z undoes. A new picture arrives at midnight: 5 by 5 on Mondays, up to 10 by 10 at the weekend.
