    How it works

    Pop in once a day. Tap each animal and offer it one of three things.

    What they love depends on their mood, and each has tastes of its own. Two hearts for a favourite, one for something they like.

    Hearts make friends. Each new level of friendship adds a card to your book, and as your friendships grow, someone new turns up at the gate.

    Moods change at midnight and are the same for everyone, so you can compare notes with friends. Missing a day only costs your streak.
