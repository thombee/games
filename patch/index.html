How to play

Plant one seedling in each row, each column and each coloured bed. Seedlings never touch, not even at corners.

Tap a square once to mark it ✕ (not here), again to plant a seedling 🌱, and again to clear it. Drag across squares to mark them all ✕. Anything that breaks a rule turns red.

Every garden can be worked out without guessing. Stuck? A hint plants one seedling for you.

Keyboard: arrow keys move between squares, Space or Enter changes a square, Delete clears it, and Ctrl+Z undoes. A new garden arrives at midnight: small and gentle on Monday, big and hard by Sunday.
